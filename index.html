How it started

Every mohalla has one.

You know the one — that stall everyone calls by a name instead of a brand. "Falane ki chai," they'd say. So-and-so's chai. Ours started exactly like that: a kettle, a bench, and a few regulars who kept showing up.

We never got around to picking a fancier name. Turns out we didn't need to — the chai did the talking, and the name just stuck.

Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

What are your timings?
We're pouring from 7 AM to 11 PM, every single day of the week — including holidays, because chai doesn't take a day off.
Do you deliver, or is it takeaway/dine-in only?
All three. Grab a bench and stay a while, take it to go, or call us on 08318170758 to arrange a delivery nearby.
Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?
Everything on the board is vegetarian. For vegan, ask us to make your cup with a plant-based milk — we keep it on hand.
Do you take card and UPI payments?
Yes — cash, card, and UPI are all welcome. No minimum order, no fuss.
Can I pre-order for pickup?
Absolutely. Call ahead on 08318170758 and your order will be ready in a kulhad by the time you walk in.
Can I book the space for a small get-together?
We can hold the bench for small groups after 8 PM. Give us a call to check availability for your date.
Do you serve snacks alongside the chai?
Always. Ask what's fresh off the tawa that day — it changes, but it's never boring.

Still have a question? Call us directly — we actually pick up.
